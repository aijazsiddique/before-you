Start tonight

Three small steps are enough for your first evening.

  1. If tonight is a hard night, start with the safety page — page 5 of the guide. It comes before everything else, on purpose.
  2. Do the two-minute three-line note (Chapter 1). It separates today's incident from tomorrow's decision — and gives you permission to stop for the night.
  3. Pick one script from Chapter 4 and save it where you can find it. One factual sentence you can send tomorrow is a real start.

That's enough for tonight. The rest of the guide — the pressure map, the capacity page, the repair test, your financial runway and the 30-day plan — can wait for daylight.
